# About photo collage

Original sources are `about-portrait.jpg` (1080 × 1440), `about-life-01.jpg` (1536 × 2048), and `about-life-02.jpg` (2304 × 4096). Byte-identical public originals live in `public/about/`. `scripts/prepare-about-photos.py` uses Pillow to make responsive WebP delivery copies at quality 90, without filters or retouching. Source files remain unchanged. `<picture>` selects responsive WebP images with the original JPEG as Next Image fallback.

`AboutPhotoCollage.tsx` keeps exactly three photo buttons in a stable DOM order. A slot permutation identifies the featured, first supporting, and second supporting positions. Selecting a smaller photo swaps only its slot and the featured slot. Motion's existing `layout` projection animates the measured rectangles for 560ms using cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). No layoutId crossfade, duplicate photo layers, slideshow, or new dependency is used. Identical 3:4 frame ratios avoid nonuniform scaling during swaps; object positions preserve faces, including the taller second life photo. The collage stage reserves its height, so surrounding content stays stationary.

The default is a centered large portrait flanked by differently positioned supporting photos. Mobile places the featured image above both supporting photos. Semantic buttons, descriptive image alt text, aria-pressed, visible focus, and a polite status announcement support keyboard and assistive technology. Reduced motion disables position/hover animation; swapping still works instantly. Fine pointers get a subtle 1.02 image hover scale. Touch requires no hover.

The About page retains its existing biography, specialties, and contact component, and includes the two introductory lines supplied in this request. Verified employment and education data were not present in the repository or its source notes; no CV details were invented or placeholder employers displayed. Existing header/footer `/about/` links are unchanged.

Browser verification covered repeated swaps in both supporting positions, the unchanged third photo, intermediate animated frames, rapid selections, keyboard Space/Enter, focus, touch, reduced motion, original image identity, and fixed stage/surrounding content. Six viewport widths (320/390/768/1024/1440/1920) were checked for photo visibility, layout bounds, full-bleed Castle crop, eye alignment, unchanged metadata/card dimensions/footer spacing, navigation, and console errors.
